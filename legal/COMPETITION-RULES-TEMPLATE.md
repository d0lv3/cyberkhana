# Competition Rules — template

**For ambassadors.** Copy this for each competition you run, fill in every
`[SQUARE BRACKET]`, delete what does not apply, and post it as an announcement
before the competition opens.

Keep it short. These get read on a phone, five minutes before the start, by
someone who is already nervous. Anything longer than one screen does not get
read, and rules nobody read are rules you cannot enforce.

**Three things you must not change:**

1. You cannot loosen section 6 of the Terms of Service. A competition can make
   scope tighter, never wider.
2. You cannot promise a prize we have not agreed to.
3. You cannot allow anything the Terms of Service forbid.

Delete this box before posting.

## What is in scope

You may only test the targets named in each challenge, at the exact address the
challenge gives.

**In scope:**
- [LIST TARGET HOSTS / ADDRESSES, or: the targets named in each challenge]

**Out of scope — attacking any of these means immediate disqualification:**
- the CyberKhana Platform itself, and its API, database and admin functions
- other participants, their accounts, and their machines
- anything on the same network as a target that is not itself a named target
- [UNIVERSITY]'s own systems, network and wifi
- anything else at all

**No denial-of-service.** Do not flood, exhaust or knock over a target, even one
that is in scope. If you break a target for everyone else, you are out.

Section 6 of the Terms of Service applies in full and is the final word on scope.

## Hints and help

- Use the Platform's hint system. Hints cost points, and that is the trade.
- **Asking the ambassador for a nudge is not a thing.** They will not confirm
  guesses, tell you if you are close, or hint in DMs.
- If a challenge looks broken, say so in [CHANNEL] — do not spend an hour on
  something that is genuinely broken.

## What gets you disqualified

- Sharing a flag, or taking one from someone else.
- Submitting a flag you did not find yourself.
- Attacking anything out of scope.
- Denial-of-service against any target.
- Brute-forcing the flag submission form, or automating submissions.
- Using more than one account.
- Working with people outside your team where that is not allowed.
- Exploiting a bug in the scoring or hint system instead of solving the
  challenge. **Report it instead** — reporting earns credit, using it earns a ban.
- Interfering with another participant.

Disqualification means your solves are voided and your points removed. Serious
cases go to CyberKhana under the Terms of Service, and may be reported to the
authorities.

  support@cyberkhana.tech. Do not use it to score.

flag leaks, or the Platform goes down. We will announce it in [CHANNEL] and
explain what we did.
